SecureVault Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Summary

SecureVault stores your documents on your own phone, encrypted. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. We cannot see your documents, passwords or codes.

Data stored on your device

Documents, photos, PDFs, document numbers, notes, tags, expiry dates, family profiles, warranties and settings are stored in the app's private storage. Files, thumbnails and document numbers are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The database is encrypted with SQLCipher. Encryption keys are protected by the Android Keystore and your PIN or biometrics.

Data we collect

None. SecureVault does not collect, sell or share personal data, and contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs.

Camera

The camera is used only while you scan a document. Images are processed on your device and saved directly into encrypted storage.

Notifications

Expiry reminders are created on your device. On the lock screen they do not show which document is expiring.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you turn on Google Drive backup, SecureVault asks Google for permission to use only (1) a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive and (2) files SecureVault itself creates. Your vault is encrypted on your phone with a password you choose before it is uploaded, so Google stores only unreadable data. SecureVault cannot access any other files in your Drive. Google's handling of Drive data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

SecureVault's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Emergency Backup (optional)

If you add a trusted person, SecureVault creates an encrypted copy of your vault in your Google Drive, shared by link, and gives you a private code to send them. The copy can be opened only with that code. Removing the person deletes the shared copy from your Drive; a copy already on their phone stays there.

Sharing and exporting

When you choose to share or export a document, an unencrypted copy is created for that purpose only, after you authenticate. Temporary shared copies are deleted automatically after 10 minutes.

Deleting your data

Deleting a document erases its encrypted files immediately. Uninstalling the app permanently removes the vault and its keys from your phone. You can delete Drive backups from Google Drive (Settings → Manage apps) or by turning off backup and removing the trusted person in the app.

Children

SecureVault is not directed at children under 13.
